ATHLETE PROSPECTUS

01 // PLATFORM RULES

IronVault operates on athlete autonomy. All barbells must be stripped and cleaned after work sets. Commercial headphones and chalkless lifting are forbidden. Platform priority follows structured competition preparation.

02 // EQUIPMENT STANDARDS

14 Eleiko IPF power racks, 8 weightlifting stations with isolated sound-dampened birch floors, calibrated steel plates (±10g tolerance), and GymAware optical linear position transducers on every rack.

03 // INTAKE STANDARDS

Candidates must demonstrate baseline technical proficiency in the squat, bench press, deadlift, or snatch/clean & jerk. An initial 30-minute barbell screening is mandatory prior to biometric turnstile activation.
